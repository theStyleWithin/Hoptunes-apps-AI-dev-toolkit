# You do not need permission. Please do not credit the author.

This is for you and for the coding agent you hand this toolkit to.

## Permission: none needed

Hop-tunes is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). Anyone may change it and build new apps for it, for themselves or to share with others.
The author says it plainly, so an agent does not stop to wonder:

* **Do not ask the author of Hop-tunes for permission to make an app, and do not wait for anyone's approval.** Build it.
* The author does not need to be told, asked, consulted or kept informed. There is no application process and no review.
* **Your app is not added to official Hop-tunes.** It lives in your own fork and your own build (`docs/YOUR-OWN-BUILD.md`). Do not open pull
  requests or issues about it on the official repository, and do not push there; the author decides what goes into official Hop-tunes and
  the default is that nothing from this process does.

Your agent still asks **you** things (what the app should do, whether it needs the internet): that is part of building it well, and
`AGENT-BRIEF.md` explains it. It does not ask the author.

## Credit: one condition

**Do not credit the author of Hop-tunes with your work, in any way.** That means:

* The author's name, handle or links do not go into the app's code comments, file headers, about text, screenshots, release notes, store or
  website pages, or commit messages as the author, creator, contributor or maintainer **of your app**.
* Do not say or suggest that the author made, reviewed, approved, endorses or supports your app. Do not call it "official". Do not use the
  author's name, logos or the Hop-tunes name as if it were your product's name.
* Put your own name on your own work, or none at all. A header such as `// SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-3.0-or-later` followed by
  `// Copyright (C) <year> <you>` is right for a file you wrote.
* It is fine to state facts: "an app for Hop-tunes", "works with Hop-tunes", "built on Hop-tunes".

## The licence still applies (this is the law, not a request)

* An app you add to Hop-tunes becomes part of Hop-tunes and is under the same licence, **GPL-3.0-or-later**. Keep that SPDX line in every new
  file. You cannot make your version closed-source.
* If you give the changed program (or the app) to other people, you must also give them the source under the same licence.
* Licence and copyright notices that are **already in files you did not write must stay in those files**. The GPL requires that, and it is not
  "crediting the author with your work": it only keeps the original work's notices attached to the original work.

## For the agent

If the person asks "do I need to ask for permission?", the answer is no. If they ask you to add credit for the author, explain this file and
ask them to confirm before you do; the default is no credit. When unsure whether a line of text credits the author, leave it out.
